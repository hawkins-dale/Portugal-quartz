A longtime Rick Steves tour hotel located on a quiet side street just off Avenida da Liberdade.

Hotel Lisboa Plaza - Avenida, a Lisbon Heritage Collection is a 4-star, family-owned historic hotel located at Tv. do Salitre 7 in the Santo António neighborhood of central Lisbon, Portugal. Overview & Vibe

- **Atmosphere:** Blends old-world Portuguese charm with an unpretentious, welcoming feel, designed by decorator Graça Viterbo. - **Location:** Just steps off the upscale Avenida da Liberdade and a 2-minute walk from Avenida Metro Station.
