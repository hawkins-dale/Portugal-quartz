## Ribeira

touristy but cool

## Gaia (Vila Nova de Gaia)

the other side of the river
World of Wine is interesting

## CEDOFEITA

best:  romantic, nice shops and cafés.  Walk everywhere
Drawback: some streets are noisy.   Travesa de Cedofeita is noisy

## BONFIM

Expats jam the cafés (in a good way).  Tem minutes walk from downtown.  Well-connected, two subways.  Walk to São Bento.  Loads of corree.  Hip area.
Close to the nightlife district.  So it can e nois at night and on the weekend, near the Praça Dos Poveiros

## RIBIERA

The riverside.  Tiny colorful houses.  BY the famous iron brige.  Tram line #1.  Flat! Palacio da Bolsa.
Disadvantages: very touristy.  Restaurants and cafés are more expensive.  15-20 minutes climb to center city.

## DOWNTOWN

Central.  Lots of transport.  Most monuments are here.  Coolest bars and restaurants.
Disadvantages: lots of tourists and traffic.  Not exactly quiet.

## Marquês

No famous stuff.  But nice parks.  Waaaaaay less hookers than before.  20 minutes flat walk to downtown.  Lots of subways. Convenient to the highwayt for day trips.
Disadvantages:  no great bars, restaurants, cafés

## Boavista

Casa de musica.  Well=-connected to city center
Problem: lots of construction.  but maybe it's done?

## Cais de Gaia

Super touristy.  No good connections.  But cute!
