**Serralves** is a cultural institution It includes a [contemporary art](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Contemporary_art "Contemporary art") museum, a park, and a villa, with each one of these being an example of contemporary architecture, [Modernism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Modernism "Modernism"), and [Art Deco](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Art_Deco "Art Deco") architecture.
