### Food

People start eating dinner at 7:30, and don't really start until 8:00 or 9:00.
**Make a res** if there's a particular place.\
But if you show up at 7:00, or even 7:30, you may have a chance.

_Couvert_  is a few Euros.  You can say no.  Each restaurant is dfifferent.

The food is fresh, simple, and not overloaded with sauces.

**DRINK ALL THE LOCAL WINE**:  the 5€ bottles are great

Gazela: cash only.  Hot dog!

Conga: mostly locals.  Fantastic sandwiches

Casa Guedes: pork sammy with goat cheese

Terra Plana for cocktails

Capela Incomun: wine bar

Main market: Mercado do Bolhão.  Touristy but still a lot of great stuff

\~~Cafe Majestic:~~  NO.  Overpriced tourist trash

#### Eat this:

- Sheep cheese! _Queijo De Ovelha Amanteigado_  ("kay-zho")
  Soft 'n' buttery.  Possibly the best cheese on Earth
- _Pão de Rio Maior_: put some o' that sheep cheese on
- _Moelas_: gizzards!
- _Leitão_. fuckin' **suckling pig**! (c.f. Filipino _lechón_)
- _Alhiera_:  chicken/turkey smoked snausages
- _Carne de porco à alentejana_ or: **Black Pork with Clams** from the black pigs
- _Percebes_ fuckin **barnacles**
- Seafood risotto
- _Cachorrinho_:  hot dog-ish
- _Chouriço à Bombeiro_:  firefighter hotdog!

#### Weather

**It** **will rain**.  Umbrellas are available from street vendors.  If you have an umbrella, it's fun to walk around.

#### Transport

- Porto is **dense**.  Walk everywhere.
- [[Buses in Porto]] are excellent.  Use https://stcp.pt/en/plan-a-trip
- The [[Metro in Porto]] is cool: most lines are mostly above ground
- Or ride bikes!  Lots of bike lanes. Or an [e-bike tour](https://www.bajabikes.eu/en/porto-highlights-tour/)!
- Bolt is better than Uber.  It's the local (cheaper) version.

Car rental isn't bad!
