Take advantage **of a site that is open to the public**, despite being private, to admire Porto from **one of the best vantage points**. The Belvedere de Vitoria, located in the city centre, **overlooks the River Douro, the Louís I Bridge and the great monuments** of the city of Oporto. Capture some suspended moments on top of the orange-tiled roofs and come back at the end of the day to enjoy the sunset over these **perfect landscapes**. Get your calves ready - there are at least **240 steps to climb.**
