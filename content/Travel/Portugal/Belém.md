This monument-packed neighbourhood is a must-see place.

### Getting There

#### Bus

Take bus 28 to the west (Restelo direction), which follows the coast line and provides an express service with few stops.

> [!warning]
> This advice conflicts with bus 728 to [[Jerónimos Monastery]].  I think they mean bus 728, as there IS NO BUS 28.  Just the famous tram.

**Bus 728,** which leaves from Sul e Suests, takes 23 minutes to the monastery

#### Train

Cascais suburban train (line "Cascais todos" or "Oeiras"); the express trains don't stop in Belém) to Belém and walk to the attractions.

#### Tram 15 to the west (Algés direction)

Follows the Junqueira residencial line. Check the route map inside the tram: it helps to find a right station for most famous of Belém attractions. Departs from the Praça do Comercio, which is 800m (10 minutes) from home.  56 minutes

The extensive bus network also serves Belém from various departure points around the city and can be less busy than the tram. The main monuments are well separated from each other and there is a road/rail highway between them that can only be crossed at a couple of places. In addition it is mostly open and unshaded, so be prepared for a lot of walking in the hot sun.

#### Belem Tower (Torre de Belém)

Open 10AM-5:30PM in winter, 10AM-6:30PM in summer (with the last entry allowed 30 minutes before closure). Be aware that access to the top of the tower, and intermediate floors, is via a very narrow, steep spiral staircase. This is used in both directions and is potentially unsafe (also females may be groped in the crush). Entry fee for the tower only is €5.

#### Jerónimos Monastery

Praça do Império, 1400-206 Lisboa, ☎ 21 362 00 34, [[x]]\(http://www.mosteirojeronimos.gov.pt/en/). Open 10AM-5:30PM in winter, 10AM-6PM in summer. Try to avoid the morning rush of tour parties (with pushy tour guides) as admission is slow and you could queue for a while. Free entry to the church of Santa Maria de Belem (where Vasco da Gama is buried), €18 only for the cloister of the monastery.

#### Monument to the Discoveries (Padrão dos Descobrimentos)

[[x]]\(https://padraodosdescobrimentos.pt/en/home/). There is a well-concealed pedestrian subway in line between the Monastery and the Monument. Entry fee €4.

#### CCB (Belém Cultural Center)

https://www.ccb.pt/Default/en/Homepage.
Open Tu-Su. The modern CCB holds a permanent contemporary art exhibition, from the Berardo Collection - it features works from Picasso, Dalí, Duchamp, Magritte, Andy Warhol, among others.

#### Coach Museum (Museu dos Coches)\\

http://museudoscoches.gov.pt/en/. Housed in the former riding school of the palace, don't miss the world's largest collection of coaches and royal vehicles

#### Statue to Afonso de Albuquerque

In front of the former Royal Palace of Belém, now the Presidential Palace, there is a massive statue looking out to sea, representing Afonso de Albuquerque, second ruler of Portuguese India in the early 16th century.
