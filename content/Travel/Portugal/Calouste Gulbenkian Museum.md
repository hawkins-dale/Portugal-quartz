Considered one of the best museums in Portugal, it comprises two art collections that are located in distinct buildings connected by a magnificent garden. It gathers a collection of artworks that cover more than two thousand years of Art, from Ancient Egypt to Contemporaneity.

Throughout the year, the Museum also organizes temporary exhibitions, concerts, conferences and guided tours. All-inclusive — 14€ (Collections + Exhibitions), Calouste Gulbenkian Museum — 10€ (Founder’s Collection + Modern Collection); ==half price== for visitors up to 29 and ==over 65.==

https://gulbenkian.pt/
